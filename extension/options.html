Profile Signals settings

These rules decide the color of the dot next to a LinkedIn profile name. Saving applies them at once to open LinkedIn tabs. The defaults come from rules.json.

Verdict

Each rule gives green (1 point), yellow (0.5) or red (0). The score is the weighted average of the rules that could be checked. A hard gate overrides the score: a red gate makes the profile red, a yellow gate caps it at yellow, an unchecked gate caps it at yellow.

below yellow yellow green

Collection

By default nothing is opened by itself: the dot stays grey until you click Collect now in the panel, or open More → About this member yourself. Automatic collection opens that panel on every profile you visit. It is faster, but it is automated use of LinkedIn's pages: check LinkedIn's terms before turning it on.

Rules

The order is the priority: rules are shown and checked from top to bottom. Weight is how much a rule counts in the score.

Cache

Collected profiles are kept in this Chrome profile only, keyed by the profile URL, so the extension does not open LinkedIn's menus again for a profile it already read. A cached result shows a small ring on the dot; Refresh in the panel always collects again.
